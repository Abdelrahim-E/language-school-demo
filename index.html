How a group works

8

Eight seats at most. Everyone speaks in every class. Nobody hides at the back.

10 weeks

One level takes ten weeks. It ends with a short speaking check with your teacher, not a long exam.

2 days

Homework comes back marked. Written work is returned within two working days, with notes you can use.

Before you join

Do I have to take the level check first?

No. It only saves time. If you skip it, a teacher asks you a few questions on your first visit and suggests a group.

Can I change group after I start?

Yes, during the first two weeks, if a seat is free in the group you want.

Are the classes online?

No. All groups meet in person at our school in Alexandria.

What if I miss a class?

Send us a message before the class and your teacher shares the notes and the homework with you.

Do you teach children?

Not at the moment. Our groups are for students aged 16 and over.

Ask for a place

Tell us the level and time you prefer. We call you within one working day to confirm a group.
